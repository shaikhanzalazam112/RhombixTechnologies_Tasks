# Save Every Drop Campaign 💧

## Campaign Overview

Save Every Drop is a social awareness campaign focused on water conservation and responsible water use.

## Designer

Anzal Azam Shaikh

## Campaign Message

"Every Drop Counts."

## Campaign Objective

- Raise awareness about water wastage
- Encourage responsible water use
- Promote simple water-saving habits

## Target Audience

- Students
- Families
- Young adults
- Communities

## Deliverables

- Main Awareness Poster
- Instagram Post
- Instagram Story
- Digital Banner
- Awareness Flyer
- Campaign Mockups
- Final Campaign Video

## Visual Theme

The campaign uses a blue, navy, light blue, and white color palette to represent water, freshness, cleanliness, and trust.
